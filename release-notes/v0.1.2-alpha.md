v0.1.1-alpha

- Turn on iterative goals from the Auto mode panel. The manager runs short turns when the queue needs more work or a question about the goal arrives, then waits for the next trigger.
- Independent review is queued even with Auto mode off. Cards show when they are waiting for a review slot, and completed review notes remain available after reopening the board.
- Expert stages show each execution attempt and its activity. You can follow a reply sent to a running executor in the task dialogue, and drafts awaiting an answer stay out of the run queue.
- The manager panel restores your reading position inside expanded report details. Reopening the manager chat at its saved end also shows replies that arrived while it was closed.
- Goal activity history loads in the background. In a Windows Electron test with a larger board and two restored windows, the first history request went from 1,341 ms to 356 ms.
- The project Worklog reuses verified entry IDs for unchanged journal files, reducing rereads when saving new entries.
- The READMEs link to setup, contribution, accessibility and private vulnerability reporting guides.
