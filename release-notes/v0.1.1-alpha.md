v0.1.0-alpha

- Back up and restore boards. Completed tasks can reopen with their saved Codex chats.
- Drafts awaiting clarification are marked on the board. Confirm a draft is ready from its task chat.
- The manager's conversation stays separate from background task and goal activity. Task replies use timestamped bubbles, and generated images stay with the manager turn that produced them.
- The board preserves its scroll position when revealing tasks, shows queued tasks with an hourglass, and animates checklist progress.
- Sign in to Codex with an API key. The app distinguishes saved credentials from verified sign-in and retains work scheduled to continue after a provider quota resets.
- The settings menu shows available updates. Installed Windows clients can download a verified update and install it on restart, with a retry when cleanup fails.
